# Code of Conduct

HackTrack welcomes people of all backgrounds and experience levels. Please help keep collaboration respectful and useful.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and constructive in issues, reviews, and discussions.
- Assume good intent, ask for clarification, and focus criticism on the work.
- Respect different viewpoints and experience levels.
- Protect private information shared during collaboration.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, personal attacks, sexualized attention, doxxing, and repeated disruptive conduct are not tolerated.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub's private contact options. Include relevant links and context. Maintainers will review reports, protect confidentiality where practical, and may remove content, warn participants, or restrict participation.

This code of conduct applies to repository spaces and project-related community interactions.
